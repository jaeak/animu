Using Animu

Play an anime song on Spotify and its opening animation loops in the background while you listen.

  1. Connect to Spotify. Spotify Premium needed for playback controls.
  2. Play a song in Spotify on any device. The matching anime opening is found on AnimeThemes and starts playing. Songs without one show their album cover instead.
Controls
  • Play / pausePlay button or Space
  • Jump within the songDrag the timeline
  • Background brightnessSlider on the right: up is brighter, down is darker

The first time a song's animation is shown it can take a couple of seconds to load; after that it's instant. Keep the server window open while you use the app.
